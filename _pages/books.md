## Recommended reading

Books I keep going back to, at the intersection of topology, data science, and machine learning.

<div class="row row-cols-1 row-cols-md-2 g-3 mb-4">
<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title"><a href="https://bookstore.ams.org/mbk-69/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Computational Topology</a></h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Herbert Edelsbrunner & John Harer</strong> — The foundational textbook on computational topology, covering simplicial complexes, homology, and persistent homology.</p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title"><a href="https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~ghrist/notes.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Elementary Applied Topology</a></h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Robert Ghrist</strong> — A beautifully illustrated introduction to applied topology, from sensor networks to robotics to data analysis. <em>Free PDF on the author's page.</em></p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-09-01249-X" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Topology and Data</a></h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Gunnar Carlsson</strong> — The seminal paper that launched topological data analysis as a field. Essential reading for anyone working at the intersection of topology and ML. <em>Bull. AMS, 2009.</em></p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title"><a href="http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction</a></h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Richard Sutton & Andrew Barto</strong> — The definitive textbook on reinforcement learning, covering bandits through deep RL and policy gradient methods. <em>Free PDF on the author's site.</em></p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title"><a href="https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Topology+for+Computing+Zomorodian" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Topology for Computing</a></h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Afra Zomorodian</strong> — Bridges discrete mathematics and algebraic topology with algorithms, covering homology computation and persistence. <em>Cambridge University Press, 2005.</em></p>
</div></div></div>
</div>
