## Recommended reading

Books that have shaped my thinking at the intersection of topology, data science, and machine learning.

<div class="row row-cols-1 row-cols-md-2 g-3 mb-4">
<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title">Computational Topology</h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Herbert Edelsbrunner & John Harer</strong> — The foundational textbook on computational topology, covering simplicial complexes, homology, and persistent homology.</p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title">Elementary Applied Topology</h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Robert Ghrist</strong> — A beautifully illustrated introduction to applied topology, from sensor networks to robotics to data analysis.</p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title">Topology and Data</h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Gunnar Carlsson</strong> — The seminal paper that launched topological data analysis as a field. Essential reading for anyone working at the intersection of topology and ML.</p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title">Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction</h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Richard Sutton & Andrew Barto</strong> — The definitive textbook on reinforcement learning, covering bandits through deep RL and policy gradient methods.</p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="col"><div class="card hoverable h-100"><div class="card-body">
<h5 class="card-title">Topology for Computing</h5>
<p class="card-text"><strong>Afra Zomorodian</strong> — Bridges discrete mathematics and algebraic topology with algorithms, covering homology computation and persistence.</p>
</div></div></div>
</div>
